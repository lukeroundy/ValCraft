# ValCraft 0.15.1

Norse pixel art, capes with their own slot, recipes for everything, per-family mob sounds, and a smarter launcher. Protocol v26 (EvCape 77 is additive).

## Download
- **ValCraft-Installer.exe** (Windows): installs or updates everything, including the launcher.
- `ValCraft-Launcher.exe`: the launcher. It updates itself and the mod files.
- `valcraft-0.15.1.jar`: the Minecraft mod (Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.259).
- `ValCraft.Valheim.dll`: the Valheim plugin.

## Changes
- All Valheim items in Minecraft were redrawn as crisp 16x16 pixel art, and the worn armour now matches the Valheim sets (fur trims, horns, hoods, capes).
- New cape slot in the inventory (no Curios needed). Valheim capes have stats, render on your back, and show on your Viking in Valheim.
- Every Valheim weapon, armour piece, shield and cape can be crafted, with recipe-book unlocks. Costs follow Valheim's materials.
- Mob sounds per family: choose in the launcher whether you hear the Minecraft sound or the Valheim stand-in.
- Launcher: silent auto-update, a one-click world backup (it also offers one before the first launch), a settings page, Steam/Epic detection with an install prompt, and a banner when a Valheim update is out.

## Notes
- The installer and launcher are unsigned, so SmartScreen may warn: click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
