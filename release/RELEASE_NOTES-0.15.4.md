# ValCraft 0.15.4

Leather scraps are no longer needed for crafting: recipes use regular Minecraft leather. Protocol v27 (unchanged).

## Download
- **ValCraft-Installer.exe** (Windows): installs or updates everything, including the launcher.
- `ValCraft-Launcher.exe`: the launcher. It updates itself and the mod files.
- `valcraft-0.15.4.jar`: the Minecraft mod (Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.259).
- `ValCraft.Valheim.dll`: the Valheim plugin.

## Changes
- The 42 ValCraft recipes that used leather scraps (rag and leather armour, capes, shields, the hammer, knives, maces and more) now take `minecraft:leather`, with the same counts. Their recipe-book unlocks trigger when you pick up leather.
- The leather scraps item is still in the game.

## Notes
- The installer and launcher are unsigned, so SmartScreen may warn: click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
